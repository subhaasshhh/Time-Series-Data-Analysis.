Analyzing the Behavior of Stock Market Movement(s) Has Often Been an Area Of Interest to Machine Learning & Time-Series Data Analyst.  It Has Been Very Challenging Due to Immense Complex Nature, Chaotic, & Dynamic Environment. With the Advent of Machine Learning & Deep Learning Algorithm(s), This Paper Tries to Significantly Reduce the Risk of Trend Prediction. This Study Compares Model(s) On Time – Series Data for Predictive Analysis i.e. SARIMA [Seasonal Auto-Regressive Integrated Moving Average], CNN [Convolutional Neural Network], & LSTM [Long Short-Term Memory] for Predicting Nifty-500 Indices Trend. The Result(s) Obtained are Promising & the Evaluation Illuminates the Power of Deep Learning Through CNN & LSTM But Also Challenges the S-ARIMA Model, Making a Great Impact On the Machine Learning Paradigm.
