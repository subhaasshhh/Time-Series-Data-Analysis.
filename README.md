Analysing the Behavior of Stock Market Volatility Has Often Been an Area of Interest to Time-Series Data Analyst as It Has Been Very Challenging Due to Immense Complex Nature, Chaotic, & Dynamic Environment. With the Scrutiny of Machine Learning & Deep Learning Algorithm(s), This Paper Tries to Significantly Reduce the Risk of Trend Prediction. This Study Compares Model(s) On Time – Series Data for Predictive Analysis i.e. S-ARIMA [Seasonal Auto-Regressive Integrated Moving Average], CNN [Convolutional Neural Network], & LSTM [Long Short-Term Memory] for Predicting Nifty-500 Indices Trend. The Result(s) Obtained are Promising & the Evaluation Illuminates the Power of Deep Learning Through CNN & LSTM Challenging the S-ARIMA Model, Making a Great Impact On the Machine Learning Paradigm.
